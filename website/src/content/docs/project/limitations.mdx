title: Known limitations
description: What choropleth-kit does not do yet, or does with caveats.
---

This is the list of limits we know about today. Each one that is planned has a link to its document in the repository.

## Interaction

- **Accessibility is not complete.** The map has `role="img"`, keyboard zoom and drill-up, and semantic breadcrumbs. Arrow-key navigation between regions, an alternative data table and live announcements are not there yet. See [Accessibility](/guides/accessibility/).
- **Clicking a marker does not fly to it.** Click-to-zoom is planned; until then a marker's click is up to your code.

## Labels and markers

- **Marker labels do not avoid region names.** They avoid each other, but a marker label can sit on top of a region name.
- **Markers are not clustered.** Many markers in a small area overlap. Clustering is planned.

## Rendering

- **SVG rendering.** The map is drawn as SVG, which is not the right tool for millions of points. See [Why choropleth-kit?](/why/).
- **The dot-matrix renderer** (`dots`) does not cap its zoom yet: dots grow large when you zoom in a lot, and it does not draw ocean dots, drill-down levels or "no data" patterns.
- **No `animation` prop yet.** Apart from the zoom flights, the drill reveal and the marker pulse (CSS, off with reduced motion), changes between states are instant.

## Packaging

- **ESM only.** There is no CommonJS build. See [What to import from where](/imports/).
- **The bundle is not tiny.** The geodata and the d3 modules add up. See [Performance model](/concepts/performance/).
- **Pre-1.0.** The API may change between minor versions. See [Status and roadmap](/project/status/).
