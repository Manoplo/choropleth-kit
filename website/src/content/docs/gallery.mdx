Every map here is **live**, and the code below it is the **file that is running**. They all use one synthetic world dataset (`sales` from this site's `data/demo.ts`): one row per country with `{ country, sales, growth, zone }`. The numbers are generated deterministically and mean nothing.

For small, self-contained examples you can paste as they are, see [Recipes](/recipes/).

## Drill-down

Click the United States to go from the world to its states. The states are loaded on demand: the bundler puts them in their own file, so they cost nothing until the user drills in. Use the breadcrumb, or `Escape`, to go back.
