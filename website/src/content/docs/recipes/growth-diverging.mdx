## How it works

- `variant="diverging"` uses two hues around a neutral color. The default palette is `rdbu`.
- `midpoint={0}` is where the neutral color sits. Without `domain`, the range is symmetric around it, so +15 and −15 get equally strong colors.
- `formatValue` adds the sign (`+8.4 %`), which the tooltip and legend both use.

## Variations

- Another palette: `palette="rdylgn"` for a red-to-green reading (check it with your audience; red and green are hard for some viewers).
- Your own colors: `palette={{ negative: "#dc2626", neutral: "#f5f5f5", positive: "#2563eb" }}`.
- A target other than zero: set `midpoint` to it, for example `midpoint={100}` for an index.
- A fixed, asymmetric range: add `domain={[-20, 40]}`. It is used as given, and `midpoint` must lie inside it.
