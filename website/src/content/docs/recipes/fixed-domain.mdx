**Problem:** by default the color range comes from your data, so "the darkest country" on one map is not the same value as on another. For a share or a rate you want a range that means something.

## How it works

- `domain={[0, 100]}` replaces the data's range. 0 % is always the first color and 100 % the last, on any map you draw with it.
- `palette={{ from, to }}` is your own two-color ramp. Choose ends with different lightness; a white-to-blue ramp is the safe choice.
- `classification={{ method: "linear" }}` gives a continuous gradient instead of classes.
- `legend={{ variant: "bar" }}` draws the legend as a bar, which suits a continuous scale. The ends show `0 %` and `100 %` through `formatValue`.

## Variations

- Steps instead of a gradient: `classification={{ method: "quantize", classes: 5 }}` splits `[0, 100]` into five equal bands.
- Your own breaks: `classification={{ method: "threshold", thresholds: [25, 50, 75] }}`.
- Perceptually even steps: add `space: "lab"` to the palette.
